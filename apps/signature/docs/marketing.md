# MIREKU — marketing notes

MIREKU 1.1.0 is in **testing**: a 14-day test build goes to testers, and the release
build is ready but unsigned on Windows. Every line below describes something 1.1.0
does today. The sounds are James Mireku's own; his name and likeness are used with his
agreement as the client, and nothing here should be sent out without his sign-off.

## In one line

James Mireku's keyboard sounds in one instrument, built for the stage.

## Short description (store card, 2 sentences)

MIREKU is James Mireku's sound library as a playable instrument: 78 of his electric
pianos, synths, FM keys, strings, pads and choirs, four at a time, with his own
performances ready to play. A setlist with scenes, a super knob and seamless switching
make it an instrument for the stage as much as the studio.

## Description (product page)

**James Mireku's sounds.** 78 instruments recorded and voiced by James Mireku, in four
worlds: 17 in JM SET (his electric pianos), 8 in MKS, 3 in FM and 50 in STRINGS, which
holds his strings, pads, choirs, brass and ambient sounds. Each world has its own
controls: Bark, Tine, Body, Tremolo and Space on JM SET; Chorus Mode, Chorus Depth,
Width, Ensemble, Warmth and Air on MKS; Brightness, Attack, Glass, Chorus Depth and Echo
on FM; Swell, Ensemble, Shimmer, Warmth and Motion on STRINGS.

**Four at once.** PERFORM plays four instruments together, A to D. Each has its own
volume, pan and on switch, and its back holds its own octave and key range, so two
instruments can be layered or split across the keyboard. Transpose, octave, solo or
poly, and four macro knobs sit underneath. James's 22 performances come with the
library, ready to play.

**Change sound without a gap.** Loading another performance never cuts the sound. Notes
you are holding, or holding with the pedal, keep sounding from the previous instrument
until you let them go, and the effects hand over without a click. New notes go to the
new sound.

**A setlist for the service or the show.** Songs in order, each with the performance
it plays; drag a song to reorder the show, step through it with PREV and NEXT, or send
a MIDI program change. Each song keeps scenes, such as verse, chorus and bridge. A
scene remembers which instruments are on, every knob, the effects, the macros and the
super knob, and a tap brings it all back while held notes keep sounding. The SUPER KNOB
moves every macro linked to it, each over its own range.

**Shape it further.** Macros, a modulation matrix, three LFOs and an arpeggiator open
in one page in place of the effects rack, with tabs to move between them. Modulation
sources are the macros, the LFOs, velocity, aftertouch, the mod wheel, expression, key
position and random. The arpeggiator plays up, down, up/down, down/up, as played,
random or chord, on any of the four instruments.

**Sixteen effects.** Each instrument has its own rack, in any order: JM Chorus,
Tremolo/Auto-Pan, Phaser, Flanger, EP Preamp/Drive, Vintage EQ, Compressor, Tape
Delay, Digital Delay, Plate Reverb, Hall/Shimmer, Widener/Ensemble, Tape Machine,
Modulator, JM Modulation and JM Rotary, a rotating speaker for organs.

**Played the way you play.** Five velocity curves, with depth and offset per sound.
MIDI learn on any knob. Pitch bend and the mod wheel move the on-screen wheels. Each
world can go to its own stereo output, OUT 1 to OUT 4, in the standalone and in a DAW.

**Find a sound by what it is.** Every sound is tagged by instrument, mood and use, so a
search for "warm pad" finds a sound named neither. Favourites, and your own presets
beside James's.

**The library is sealed.** Each instrument is one encrypted file that only MIREKU can
open. Your presets, performances and setlists are saved the same way, as .jmi files.

**Your music stays on your computer.** One licence key, typed once. MIREKU then works
with no internet for up to 30 days at a time, and contacts amanorsac.studio only to
renew the licence. No audio, presets or usage data are ever sent.

## Features (bullet list)

- 78 instruments by James Mireku: 17 JM SET, 8 MKS, 3 FM, 50 STRINGS
- 22 performances by James Mireku
- PERFORM: four instruments at once, each with its own octave and key range
- Seamless switching: held notes keep sounding when the performance changes
- Setlist: drag to reorder, PREV and NEXT, MIDI program change
- Scenes per song: instruments, knobs, effects, macros and super knob in one tap
- Super knob: one control over every linked macro, each with its own range
- Four macros, modulation matrix, three LFOs and an arpeggiator in one edit page
- Sixteen effects per instrument, in any order, including JM Rotary
- Five velocity curves; MIDI learn; expression (CC 11) on the master level
- Four stereo outputs, in the standalone and in a DAW
- Search and filter by instrument, mood and use
- Sealed library; presets, performances and setlists saved as .jmi
- Standalone application, VST3 and Audio Unit

## Words we use, and words we do not

- Say what it does: "keeps sounding", "brings it all back", "only MIREKU can open".
- British spelling: favourite, colour, licence.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- No numbers we have not measured, no comparisons with other products or keyboards, no testimonials.
- "Sealed" or "encrypted", never "uncrackable": a key inside an app can be extracted by someone determined.
